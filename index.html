THE CORE IDEA

Vision Sense AI vs Conventional

The difference is simple: the conventional classroom powers the whole room, while Vision Sense AI responds to where students actually are.

01CONVENTIONAL CLASSROOM

Whole-room operation

Fans and lights are typically switched on for the classroom as a whole, even when only part of the room is being used.

  • 2 zones: both zones can be powered together.
  • Occupancy: no zone-specific detection.
  • Control: manual switching.
  • Energy use: unused zones may continue consuming power.
Student in Zone 1→Whole classroom powered
02VISION SENSE AI

Zone-aware operation

The camera-based system identifies where a student is present and controls each zone independently.

  • 2 zones: Zone 1 and Zone 2 are controlled separately.
  • Occupancy: person detection determines the occupied zone.
  • Temperature: fans respond to the DHT22 cooling condition.
  • Energy use: only the required zone devices are operated.
Student in Zone 1→Zone 1 fan + light ON
